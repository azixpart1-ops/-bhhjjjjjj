Aspen is an orthopaedic chaise dog bed for small and medium dogs, with a profiled egg-crate foam core, a bolstered edge with one open corner, a fleece sleeping surface and a waterproof lining.

What you get

  • One open corner. A chaise-style entry they can walk straight into.
  • Bolstered perimeter. An edge to lean into almost all the way round.
  • Egg-crate foam core. Profiled foam spreads their weight rather than compressing flat.
  • Fleece and waterproof lining. Soft fleece where they sleep, and a waterproof lining around the foam.

Best for

Small and medium dogs who would rather walk into a bed than step over a side, and like a head rest once down.

Size and fit

One size, for small and medium dogs. The bolster takes up part of the outside footprint, so measure your dog curled up as well as stretched out. Ask us for exact measurements before you order.

Care

Follow the sewn-in care label on the cover. Wipe the lining. Air-dry fully before refitting. Never wash or soak the foam.

A bed is for comfort, not treatment. If your dog is in pain, recovering or finding it hard to move, ask your vet about the right place for them to rest.
