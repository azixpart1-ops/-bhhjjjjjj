Your dog gets their own seat beside yours, with memory foam underneath and a cushioned arm on three sides. The soft seat and the armrest are why they end up on your sofa. Pine gives them both, so you get your cushions back.

Pine is a memory foam dog sofa with cushioned bolsters on three sides and an open front, in four sizes from Medium to XXL. The whole cover unzips to wash.

What you get

  • Memory foam seat. They settle onto a memory foam base instead of the floor or your sofa.
  • Three arms to lean on. Cushioned bolsters on three sides for a chin, a back or a sideways flop.
  • An open front. Nothing to climb over, so stepping in is easy at any age.
  • Full-zip cover. The whole cover comes off for washing, following the sewn-in care label.

Best for

Dogs who lean on the sofa arm, from medium breeds up to giant breeds stretched out on the XXL.

Measure your dog lying on their side, nose to base of tail, and add 15–20cm. The bolsters take up some of the outside measurements, so between sizes, size up. Not sure? Ask us with their breed and weight.

Care

Unzip the cover and wash it following the sewn-in care label. Air-dry it fully before refitting. Never wash or soak the foam.

A bed is for comfort, not treatment. If your dog is in pain, recovering or finding it hard to move, ask your vet about the right place for them to rest.
