Your dog can prop their chin on the bolster, then stretch right out without ever leaving the bed. It has what a cheap bed leaves out: profiled foam, a waterproof membrane over it, a heavy-duty zip you will use, and a base that doesn't skate.

Blackthorn is an orthopaedic bolster dog bed for large dogs, with a profiled egg-crate foam base, a waterproof inner membrane, a zipped plush cover and a non-slip base. Large and XL, in Grey or Dark Grey.

What you get

  • Chin-rest bolster. The raised bolster gives their chin somewhere to rest without enclosing every side.
  • Egg-crate foam base. Profiled foam cushions the whole sleeping surface.
  • Waterproof membrane. Sits between the plush cover and the foam, so a spill or wet coat stays off the foam.
  • Heavy-duty zip. The plush cover comes off for washing, with a zip you will actually use.
  • Non-slip base. Helps keep the bed in place on smooth floors.

Best for

Large dogs who switch between leaning against an edge and stretching right out.

Sizes are outside measurements and the bolster takes up some of that, so between sizes, size up. Measure your dog lying on their side, nose to base of tail, and add 15–20cm.

Care

Unzip the cover and wash it following the sewn-in care label. Air-dry fully before refitting. Never wash or soak the foam.

A bed is for comfort, not treatment. If your dog is in pain, recovering or finding it hard to move, ask your vet about the right place for them to rest.
