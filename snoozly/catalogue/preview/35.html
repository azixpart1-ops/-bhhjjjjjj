A memory foam bed smart enough to sit out in the room, so your dog stays where the company is. The cover is corduroy furniture fabric, not coated nylon, over a memory foam core rather than fibre filling that packs into ridges.

Spruce is a memory foam dog bed with cushioned bolsters and a tactile corduroy cover, in Small, Medium and Large and in Beige, Grey or Green. The cover comes off and washes at 30°C.

What you get

  • Memory foam core. Foam that holds its shape, not fibre filling that packs down into ridges.
  • Bolsters for a chin. Cushioned sides for the dog who always sleeps against something.
  • Corduroy in three colours. A ribbed, tactile cover in Beige, Grey or Green that suits the furniture.
  • 30°C machine wash. The cover comes off and goes in the machine, following the sewn-in care label.

Best for

Small and medium dogs who sleep against something, in rooms where you'd rather not hide the bed.

Care

Unzip the cover and wash it at 30°C, following the sewn-in care label. Air-dry fully before refitting. Never wash or soak the foam.

A bed is for comfort, not treatment. If your dog is in pain, recovering or finding it hard to move, ask your vet about the right place for them to rest.
