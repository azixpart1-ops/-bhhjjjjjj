Your dog gets a quilted, cushioned floor in place of a cold, hard crate tray, with a low edge for their chin. The crate is where many dogs spend the night and much of the day, which makes this the most-used bed in the house.

Bramble is a quilted dog crate mattress with softly padded low sides and a zipped cover that washes at 30°C. It lies flat inside the crate without folding. Small, Medium and Large.

What you get

  • Quilted all over. Cushioned across the whole base, not just the middle.
  • Low padded sides. An edge for their chin, without a high wall that fights the crate door.
  • Lies flat. Cut as a proper mattress, flat enough to sit in the crate without folding, unlike a blanket.
  • 30°C washable cover. It unzips and goes in the machine.

Best for

Crates, car boots and travel: anywhere a low padded mattress needs to lie flat. It is a comfort accessory, not a vehicle restraint.
