Ash is an XL orthopaedic bolster dog bed, 122 × 89cm, with memory foam and an egg-crate profiled layer inside a waterproof lining, three cushioned sides, an open front and a short-plush cover that unzips to wash.

What you get

  • Their own sofa edge. Three bolsters with room for a resting chin or a sideways lean.
  • An open front. No full-height wall on one side, so they can walk straight in.
  • Room for a big dog. Around 70% more floor area than a typical 90 × 70cm large bed.
  • Foam kept dry. Memory foam with an egg-crate profiled layer, inside a waterproof inner lining.
  • Smooth and steady. A short-plush cover that unzips to wash, on a non-skid base that helps it stay put.

Best for

Big dogs who wedge themselves against the sofa arm to switch off, and like an edge without a wall across the front.

Size and fit

One size, XL. Measure your dog lying on their side, nose to base of tail, and add 15–20cm. The bolsters take up some of the outside footprint. Not sure? Ask us with their breed and weight.

Care

Unzip the cover and wash it following the sewn-in care label. Air-dry fully before refitting. Never wash or soak the foam.

A bed is for comfort, not treatment. If your dog is in pain, recovering or finding it hard to move, ask your vet about the right place for them to rest.
