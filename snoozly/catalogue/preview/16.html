Your dog can lie full length along the bed with their back against a bolster, instead of curling up in the middle. On a heavy dog, one thin layer of foam is not much between them and the floor. Beech has two, in a 20cm-deep bed.

Beech is an XXL memory foam bolster dog bed, 132 × 104 × 20cm, with memory foam paired with an egg-crate layer, bolsters on three sides, an open front, a fleece sleeping surface and linen-effect side panels.

What you get

  • Built to stretch out on. 132 × 104cm, so a genuinely large dog can lie full length.
  • Two foam layers. Memory foam for contour, with egg-crate foam underneath for support.
  • Three sides, open front. Somewhere to lean, and nothing to climb over.
  • Fleece and linen-effect. Soft where it matters, tidy where it shows.

Best for

Giant breeds and big dogs who sleep stretched out rather than curled up, and like a bolster at their back.

Size and fit

One size. Measure your dog lying on their side, nose to base of tail, and add 15–20cm, then measure the space in the room. Not sure? Ask us with their breed and weight.

Care

Follow the sewn-in care label on the cover. Air-dry it fully before refitting. Never wash or soak the foam.

A bed is for comfort, not treatment. If your dog is in pain, recovering or finding it hard to move, ask your vet about the right place for them to rest.
