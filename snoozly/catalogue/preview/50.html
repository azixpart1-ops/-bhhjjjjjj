Your dog gets a sheltered spot of their own in the garden, raised clear of the damp grass. Everything garden days need is built in: a raised floor off the damp, a ventilated shell and a shaped doorway that helps shed rain.

Bothy is a plastic garden dog house with a raised floor, a ventilated shell and an arched doorway. It is a garden addition for supervised days outside, not a replacement for indoor housing. Bedding is not included.

What you get

  • Off the wet ground. The raised floor keeps them clear of damp grass.
  • Sheds rain. A sloped shell and arched doorway help water run off.
  • Air moves through. Ventilation in the shell lets air pass through the house.
  • Nothing to launder. The plastic wipes clean with a damp cloth and mild soap.

Best for

Dogs who like a sheltered spot on supervised garden days. Give them water and safe shade, and bring them indoors in extreme weather: a kennel won't stop overheating.

Size and fit

Ask us for the internal floor, doorway and supported load measurements before ordering. Your dog should be able to walk in, stand, turn round and lie down comfortably inside.

Care

Wipe the shell with a damp cloth and mild soap. Follow the separate care label for any cushion you add, store soft furnishings under cover, and let everything dry before use.
