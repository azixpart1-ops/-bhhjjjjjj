Getting into bed takes nothing out of your dog: it sits low, with just enough rim to lean on. Two things a cheap bed leaves out: memory foam instead of loose filling, and a waterproof liner so one accident doesn't end the bed.

Poplar is an oval memory foam dog bed with a low profile, a raised rim for a chin and a waterproof inner liner under the zipped cover. Small, Medium and Large.

What you get

  • A low way in. It sits close to the floor instead of putting a high wall in the way.
  • A rim for their chin. Raised just enough to lean on, not enough to get stuck behind.
  • Memory foam core. Foam that holds its shape under the weight it carries.
  • Waterproof liner. It sits between the cover and the foam, so an accident doesn't reach the core.
  • Cover that unzips. Removable and washable, following the sewn-in care label.

Best for

Older dogs, stiff dogs, and any dog who pauses before climbing into their bed.

Measure your dog lying on their side, nose to base of tail, and add 15–20cm. The rim takes up part of the outside measurements, so between sizes, size up. Not sure? Ask us with their breed and weight.

Care

Unzip the cover and wash it following the sewn-in care label. Air-dry fully before refitting. Never wash or soak the foam.

A bed is for comfort, not treatment. If your dog is in pain, recovering or finding it hard to move, ask your vet about the right place for them to rest.
