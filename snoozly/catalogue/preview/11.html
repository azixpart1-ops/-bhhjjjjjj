Holly Orthopaedic Dog Sofa Bed

A wall on every side to lean into, and a low front they can step over.

Your dog gets a surround to press into whichever way they turn, and a low front step that's easy at any age. The lowered front keeps this bed useful for years, because a full wall is what an older, stiffer dog quietly stops climbing.

Holly is an orthopaedic dog sofa bed with a profiled egg-crate foam base, a bolster on all four sides, a lowered front entry, a soft flannel cover, a waterproof liner around the foam and a non-slip base.

What you get

  • A four-sided surround. Something to lean into whichever way they turn in the night.
  • A low front. The entry section is dropped, so getting in and out is easy at any age.
  • Profiled foam underneath. Egg-crate foam that spreads their weight rather than compressing flat.
  • Protected and steady. A waterproof liner around the foam, flannel on top and a non-slip base underneath.

Best for

Small and medium dogs who like walls around them, including older dogs who find a full-height wall hard to step over.

Care

Remove the cover and wash it following the sewn-in care label. Wipe the liner. Air-dry fully before refitting, and never wash or soak the foam.

A bed is for comfort, not treatment. If your dog is in pain, recovering or finding it hard to move, ask your vet about the right place for them to rest.
