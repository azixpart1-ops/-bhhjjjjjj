They get the corner they would pick anyway: back to the wall, eyes on the room, and still beside you. It turns a corner you weren't using into their spot, with memory foam and a waterproof liner under the plush.

Alder is a triangular corner orthopaedic dog bed, 97 × 97cm and 19cm deep, with a profiled memory foam base, cushioned bolsters, a waterproof inner liner and a removable short-plush cover.

What you get

  • Made for a corner. The triangular footprint sits flush in a corner or at the end of a sofa, not out in the room.
  • Memory foam base. Profiled memory foam underneath, rather than loose filling.
  • Bolsters to lean on. Cushioned bolsters give a dog who leans an edge of their own, and a natural chin-rest.
  • Waterproof under the plush. The removable short-plush cover sits over a waterproof inner liner.
  • Stays put. A silicone-dot base helps it stay in place, and a reinforced handle carries the empty bed.

Best for

Dogs who like a bolstered edge and their back to the wall, in rooms where floor space matters.

Size and fit

The two straight sides each measure 97cm, so check that against your corner. Measure your dog curled up or lying on their side, and allow for the bolsters. Not sure? Ask us with their breed and weight.

Care

Remove the cover and wash it following the sewn-in care label. Air-dry fully before refitting. Never wash or soak the foam.

A bed is for comfort, not treatment. If your dog is in pain, recovering or finding it hard to move, ask your vet about the right place for them to rest.
