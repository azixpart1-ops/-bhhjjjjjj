Damson is an upholstered orthopaedic dog sofa with a raised back, two sofa arms and a supportive memory foam base. The cover comes off and machine washes. Medium, Large and XL, in Blue, Green, Brown or Grey.

What you get

  • Memory foam base. Supportive foam where they lie, rather than loose filling that shifts and flattens.
  • A back and two arms. Raised on three sides for a chin, a back or a full sideways lean.
  • Upholstery, not nylon. Piped seams and furniture-weight fabric, in four colourways.
  • Cover comes off. Removable and machine washable, following the sewn-in care label.

Best for

Living rooms where the dog bed has to look like it belongs, and dogs who would otherwise be on your sofa.

Size and fit

Measure your dog lying on their side, nose to base of tail, and add 15–20cm. The back and arms take up part of the footprint, so between sizes, size up. Ask us for exact measurements.

Care

Remove the cover and wash it following the sewn-in care label. Air-dry fully before refitting. Never wash or soak the foam.

A bed is for comfort, not treatment. If your dog is in pain, recovering or finding it hard to move, ask your vet about the right place for them to rest.
