Your dog gets a dry, raised place to lie after a wet walk, instead of the cold hallway tiles. There's no foam, filling or cover to soak up water, hold smell or flatten. Just a metal frame and mesh you hose down.

Brook is a raised dog bed with a taut, breathable mesh deck on a metal frame, in Large, XL and XXL. No foam, no stuffing and no cover to wrestle off.

What you get

  • Clear of the floor. Air moves underneath, so heat isn't trapped between your dog and the tiles.
  • Taut mesh deck. Breathable mesh spreads their weight instead of collapsing under it.
  • Nothing to soak or shred. No foam and no loose filling to go soggy or get pulled out.
  • Hose it down. Rinse the frame and mesh and let it dry. No wash cycle, no damp bed smell.
  • Indoors or out. Conservatory, utility room, garden, kennel or campsite.

Best for

Heavy-coated breeds, muddy dogs, hot sleepers and homes with hard floors.
