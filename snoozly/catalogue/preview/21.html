Dogs who come in wet or muddy, dogs who like a raised edge for their chin or back, and homes with hard floors.

Size and fit

The bolsters take up part of the outside footprint. Measure your dog lying on their side, nose to base of tail, and add 15–20cm. Not sure which size? Ask us with their breed and weight.

Follow the sewn-in care label on the cover. The leather handles have their own care instructions. Air-dry fully before use.
