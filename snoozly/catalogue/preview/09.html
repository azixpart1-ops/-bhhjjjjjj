Rowan is a memory foam bolster dog bed: memory foam over an egg-crate layer, bolsters on three sides, an open front, a fleece sleeping surface, a zipped removable cover and a non-slip base.

What you get

  • An open front. No wall to step over, so going to bed and getting up again costs them nothing.
  • Three bolstered sides. A chin rest and a back rest, with soft fleece to lie on between them.
  • Two layers of foam. Memory foam for contour over egg-crate foam for support.
  • Stays where you put it. A non-slip base underneath, and a zipped cover that comes off to wash.

Best for

Older or stiffer dogs, and any dog who pauses at the wall of an enclosed bed but still likes something to lean on.

Size and fit

Measure your dog lying on their side, nose to base of tail, and add 15–20cm; the bolsters take up part of the outside. Ask us for the exact measurements and whether it fits.

Care

Unzip the cover and wash it following the sewn-in care label. Air-dry fully before refitting. Never wash or soak the foam.

A bed is for comfort, not treatment. If your dog is in pain, recovering or finding it hard to move, ask your vet about the right place for them to rest.
