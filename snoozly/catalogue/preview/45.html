Their own cool spot for summer: air moves underneath and takes the heat with it.

On hot days your dog gets a cool place of their own, instead of the coolest tile in the house. It cools the simple way, with airflow: no gel to puncture, no batteries to recharge, nothing inside to leak or run flat.

Breeze is a raised cooling dog bed: a taut mesh deck on a steel frame, lifted clear of the floor, in Medium, Large and XL. There's no cover to wash.

What you get

  • Airflow on all sides. Warm air moves away from under them instead of building up beneath.
  • Firm, even surface. Taut mesh takes their weight across the whole deck.
  • Nothing to go wrong. No gel, no batteries and no foam filling.
  • No cover to wash. Wipe or rinse it. There's nothing to unzip or fight back on.
  • Summer and winter. Useful in the garden all summer and in a warm kitchen all winter.

Best for

Hot sleepers, thick-coated breeds, and summers in a house with no air conditioning.
