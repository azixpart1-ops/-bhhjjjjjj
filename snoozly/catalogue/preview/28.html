What you get

  • Room to lie full length. 122 × 91cm and 18cm deep, for a large or giant breed.
  • L-shaped bolster. Cover along two sides, and a corner for their head.
  • Two open sides. No walls eating into the footprint, and nothing to step over.
  • Layered foam. An egg-crate top over layered foam, built to carry a heavy dog without compressing flat.

Best for

Large and giant breeds who like to stretch out, and big dogs who don't want to be boxed in.

Size and fit

Two sides are usable right to the edge. Measure your dog lying on their side, nose to base of tail, and add 15–20cm. Not sure? Ask us with their breed and weight.

Care

Follow the sewn-in care label on the cover. Air-dry fully before refitting. Never wash or soak the foam.

A bed is for comfort, not treatment. If your dog is in pain, recovering or finding it hard to move, ask your vet about the right place for them to rest.
