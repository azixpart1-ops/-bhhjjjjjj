Your dog can sprawl diagonally, legs out, on warm sherpa, with no sides to hem them in. Stonewashed fabric, sherpa and leather handles are the parts you touch every day, and the first cut on cheaper beds.

Moss is an open pillow dog mattress with a green stonewashed exterior, a sherpa-style sleeping surface, virgin fibre cushioning, leather handles and a removable cover that machine washes at 30°C. Medium and Large.

What you get

  • No sides at all. An open pillow they can flop onto from any direction.
  • Sherpa where they lie. A soft, warm surface over virgin fibre cushioning.
  • Soft, not firm. Fibre filled by design. If your dog needs firm support, choose a memory foam bed.
  • Leather handles. Move it one-handed from the kitchen to beside the sofa.
  • A washable cover. It comes off and goes in the machine at 30°C.

Best for

Sprawlers who sleep diagonally and change position through the night, and dogs who sleep on the floor beside a bed with sides.
