Your dog gets a nest with walls that stay up when they lean on them, not sides that fold flat. A wall that stays up is the whole point of a nest bed. Structured felt keeps these sides standing on their own.

Dove is a nest dog bed built in structured felt, with padded high sides and a cushioned base inside a properly enclosed nest shape. Small, Medium and Large.

What you get

  • Structured felt walls. They stand up on their own instead of flopping outward.

  • Cushioned base. Soft underfoot inside a defined, nest-shaped sleeping space.
