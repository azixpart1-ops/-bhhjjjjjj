A giant breed gets the whole bed, with no shoulder on the floor and no head hanging over the edge. A bed this big only works with enough foam depth and enough footprint. Hornbeam has both, plus a waterproof liner so one bad night doesn't end it.

Hornbeam is an XXL orthopaedic dog sofa bed, 132 × 104cm and 20cm deep, with a profiled egg-crate foam core, bolsters on four sides, a lowered front, a flannel sleeping cover and a waterproof liner.

What you get

  • A true XXL footprint. 132 × 104cm and 20cm deep, so a giant breed isn't hanging off the edge.
  • Lowered front. Low enough that a heavy, older dog can step in without hauling themselves up.
  • Four bolstered sides. Something to lean into on every side.
  • Egg-crate foam core. Spreads the weight of a very heavy dog rather than compressing to the floor.
  • Waterproof liner. Protects the foam from accidents and wet coats.

Best for

Giant breeds and very heavy dogs, including older ones who would rather step in than climb.

Size and fit

Measure your dog lying on their side, nose to base of tail, and add 15–20cm, then check your floor space. Not sure? Ask us with their breed and weight.

Care

Remove the cover and wash it following the sewn-in care label. Wipe the liner. Air-dry fully before refitting. Never wash or soak the foam.

A bed is for comfort, not treatment. If your dog is in pain, recovering or finding it hard to move, ask your vet about the right place for them to rest.
