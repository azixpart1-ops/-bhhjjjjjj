A giant dog fits on it, all of them, instead of folding onto a mat with one shoulder on the floor. A bed this size needs enough foam to carry a very heavy dog without sinking to the floor. This one has two layers.

Larch is an XXL memory foam bolster dog bed, 132 × 104 × 20cm, with memory foam over an egg-crate support layer, three bolstered sides, an open front, a fleece sleeping surface and navy linen-effect side panels.

What you get

  • A genuine XXL footprint. 132 × 104cm, more than twice the floor area of a typical 90 × 70cm large bed.
  • Two layers of foam. Memory foam over egg-crate foam, because a very heavy dog flattens a single thin layer.
  • Three bolstered sides. Somewhere to rest a head that weighs as much as a small dog.
  • Navy linen-effect sides. Fleece where they lie, smart navy panels where you see it.

Best for

Giant breeds such as Newfoundlands and Great Danes, and any dog who has outgrown beds sold as large.

Size and fit

One size. Measure your dog lying on their side, nose to base of tail, and add 15–20cm. The bolsters take up part of the outside, so check your floor space too. Ask us with their breed and weight.

Care

Follow the sewn-in care label on the cover. Let it air-dry fully before refitting. Never wash or soak the foam.

A bed is for comfort, not treatment. If your dog is in pain, recovering or finding it hard to move, ask your vet about the right place for them to rest.
