Best for

Size and fit

Measure your dog lying on their side, nose to base of tail, and add 15–20cm. The whole surface is usable, so there's no allowance for bolsters. Ask us for exact measurements, or which size suits their breed and weight.

Care

Remove the cover and wash it following the sewn-in care label. The leather handles have their own care instructions. Air-dry fully before refitting.

A bed is for comfort, not treatment. If your dog is in pain, recovering or finding it hard to move, ask your vet about the right place for them to rest.
