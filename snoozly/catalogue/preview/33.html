A dog who sleeps wedged into the corner of the sofa gets walls to lean into on every side. An easy way to find out if your dog is a walls dog: high cushioned sides, soft fleece, and a cover that zips off to wash.

Robin is a high-sided nest dog bed with cushioned walls around the resting area, a fleece sleeping finish, soft fibre filling and a removable zipped cover. Five sizes, Small to XXL, in Green, Grey or Bronze.

What you get

  • High cushioned sides. Walls around the whole resting area, not a low rim.
  • Fleece where they lie. A soft, warm finish to burrow into.
  • Soft fibre filling. Yielding rather than firm. If your dog needs firm support, a memory foam bed is the better choice.
  • Five sizes, three colours. Small to XXL, in Green, Grey or Bronze.
  • Zipped cover. It comes off to wash.

Best for

Dogs who curl up, burrow, or sleep wedged into the corner of the sofa, and prefer soft filling to firm foam.

Size and fit

The high walls take up a large part of the outside footprint, so measure your dog curled up rather than stretched out, and size up if between sizes. Not sure? Ask us with their breed and weight.

Care

Unzip the cover and wash it following the sewn-in care label. Air-dry fully before refitting.
