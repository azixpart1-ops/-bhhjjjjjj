What you get

  • Cream bouclé. A tactile texture that sits in the room instead of being hidden from it.
  • A rim for curling. Raised sides define their space and give a chin somewhere to rest.
  • Soft, not firm. Soft-filled rather than foam, so the nest feels cosy rather than firm.
  • 30°C washable cover. It zips off for the machine, which matters with cream.

Best for

Curled-up sleepers who like a soft nest. Dogs who need firm support are better suited to a foam bed.
