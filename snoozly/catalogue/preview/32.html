Your dog gets teddy fleece when it's cold and smooth canvas when it's warm, from the same bed. It's two beds in one, and the one you aren't using isn't taking up space in a cupboard.

Hare is a reversible square dog bed, 80 × 80cm, with teddy fleece on one face and smooth-weave canvas on the other, under a removable zipped cover.

What you get

  • Two surfaces. Teddy fleece on one side, smooth canvas on the other. Flip it with the season.
  • Square format. 80 × 80cm suits dogs who sleep curled or diagonally rather than stretched in a line.
  • No walls. They can get on from any edge and lie whichever way they like.
  • Zipped cover. It comes off for washing, following the sewn-in care label.

Best for

Dogs who burrow into anything soft in winter but head for the kitchen tiles in summer, and who sleep curled or diagonally.
