A dog who likes to feel something on every side gets a cushioned edge wherever they turn in the night. Memory foam that doesn't pack down, and a waterproof liner so one accident doesn't end it: the two parts that usually fail on a cheap bed.

Willow is a memory foam dog bed with a bolster on all four sides, a raised rim for a chin and a waterproof inner liner under a zipped cover. Large, 90 × 65cm.

What you get

  • A border on four sides. Cushioned all the way round the sleeping area, with a rim to rest a chin on.
  • Memory foam base. Foam that keeps its shape, rather than filling that packs down.
  • Waterproof inner liner. Sits between the cover and the foam.
  • Zipped cover. Unzips to wash, following the sewn-in care label.

Best for

Dogs who curl up tight, sleep pressed into corners, or settle better with something on every side.

Care

Unzip the cover and wash it following the sewn-in care label. Air-dry fully before refitting. Never wash or soak the foam.

A bed is for comfort, not treatment. If your dog is in pain, recovering or finding it hard to move, ask your vet about the right place for them to rest.
