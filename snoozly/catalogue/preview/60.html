Your dog gets a solid foam edge to rest their chin on, one that holds its shape instead of sagging. Foam in both the base and the rim. Loose filling is cheaper, but it shifts and flattens; foam keeps a defined edge.

Sycamore is an orthopaedic foam dog bed with a raised, structured foam rim around a foam base. The machine-washable cover comes off. Medium and Large, in grey.

What you get

  • A rim built from foam. A proper resting edge for a heavy head, not a floppy fabric tube.
  • Foam underneath. Cushioning under the whole sleeping area, framed by the raised surround.
  • Washable cover. It comes off and machine washes, following its care label.
  • Understated grey. Sits quietly in a living room without shouting about it.

Best for

Habitual head-resters, and dogs who like to sleep pressed against an edge.

Measure your dog lying on their side, nose to base of tail, and add 15–20cm. The raised edge takes up some of the outside measurement, so between sizes, size up. Unsure? Ask us.

Care

Remove the cover and wash it following the sewn-in care label. Air-dry fully before refitting. Never wash or soak the foam.

A bed is for comfort, not treatment. If your dog is in pain, recovering or finding it hard to move, ask your vet about the right place for them to rest.
