Your dog gets a soft plush bed, and you get their name on it every time you walk past. The name is embroidered, not printed, so it doesn't crack or peel, and it survives the washing.

Fox is a personalised dog bed with your dog's name embroidered on it, a soft textured plush surface, fibre cushioning and a removable zipped cover, in seven sizes from XS to 3XL.

What you get

  • Their name, embroidered. Stitched in rather than printed on, so it won't crack and peel.
  • Textured plush surface. Soft and textured where they lie.
  • Fibre cushioning. Soft comfort rather than firm support.
  • Zipped cover. The cover unzips and comes off to wash.

Best for

Dogs who like a soft bed, and anyone giving a gift that lands for a new puppy or a first Christmas.
