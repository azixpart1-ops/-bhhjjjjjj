A nervous dog, new rescue or puppy gets deep, soft walls to curl behind, out of sight of the room. Most nest beds are one sewn unit. Here the cushion lifts out, the covers come off, and a waterproof layer catches what gets through.

Birch is a bouclé nest dog bed with deep soft walls, a removable crumb memory foam cushion, a waterproof layer beneath the cover and machine-washable covers throughout. Small, Medium and Large.

What you get

  • Deep soft walls. High enough to curl behind, out of sight of the room.
  • Crumb memory foam cushion. It lifts out, so the base and the walls wash separately.
  • Waterproof layer. Under the cover. For puppies and older dogs, this is the part that saves the bed.
  • Textured bouclé. Soft to lie on, and at home in a room you sit in.

Best for

Nervous dogs, new rescues, puppies and older dogs who want somewhere soft, with sides to curl behind.

Size and fit

The deep walls take up a large part of the outside footprint, so measure your dog curled up and size up if you are between sizes. Not sure? Ask us with their breed and weight.

Care

Lift out the cushion, remove the covers and wash them following the sewn-in care labels. Air-dry fully before refitting.

A bed is for comfort, not treatment. If your dog is in pain, recovering or finding it hard to move, ask your vet about the right place for them to rest.
