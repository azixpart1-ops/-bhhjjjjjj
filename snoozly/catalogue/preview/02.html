Your dog rides on around 10cm of memory foam, not a hard seat, and has their own bed when you arrive. One thing does two jobs: a padded car seat on the road and a portable bed at the other end, so there is less to pack.

Swallow is a memory foam dog car seat with a base of around 10cm of memory foam, a seat-belt channel, an adjustable headrest strap and a fabric cover that unclips to wash.

What you get

  • Around 10cm of foam. Memory foam underneath them, so they are not lying on a hard car seat.
  • Sits in position. A seat-belt channel and an adjustable headrest strap position the seat, and two inside clips are supplied.
  • A bed when you arrive. It doubles as a portable bed, so they have somewhere of their own once you get there.
  • Washes after muddy trips. The fabric unclips and comes off to wash, while the foam stays out of the machine.

Best for

Small and medium dogs who fit the inside size and stated load, and owners who want one padded seat that also works as a travel bed.

Size and fit

Inside it is about 66 × 38cm, with a stated total load of about 25kg. Check your dog, car and the fitting instructions first. It is not crash-tested: use an appropriate vehicle restraint and don't rely on the clips as crash protection.

Care

Unclip the fabric and wash it as the care label says. Keep the foam out of the washing machine, and let every part dry fully before you refit it.
