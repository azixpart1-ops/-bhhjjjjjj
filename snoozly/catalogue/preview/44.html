  • Room for the full stretch. A flat, open 110 × 85cm footprint with no bolsters eating into it.
  • Foam edge to edge. No thin spot at the sides and no stuffing to shift into lumps.
  • 10cm deep. Cushions the hips and elbows a big dog rests their weight on.
  • Step on from any side. A level edge with nothing to climb over.
  • Machine-washable cover. It zips off for the washing machine. The foam stays out of the wash.

Best for

Large breeds, side sleepers and sprawlers who change position through the night.

It comes in one size. Measure your dog lying on their side, nose to base of tail, and add 15–20cm; the whole surface is usable. Not sure it fits? Ask us with their breed and weight.

Care

Unzip the cover and wash it following the sewn-in care label. Air-dry fully before refitting. Never wash or soak the foam.

A bed is for comfort, not treatment. If your dog is in pain, recovering or finding it hard to move, ask your vet about the right place for them to rest.
