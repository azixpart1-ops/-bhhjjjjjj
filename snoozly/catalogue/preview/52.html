Your dog walks up a ramp into a raised garden retreat, clear of the wet grass and mud. A raised floor and an access ramp in one kennel: your dog stays off the mud and walks up instead of stepping up.

Lodge is a raised plastic dog kennel for the garden, with an off-ground floor, an arched doorway and an access ramp to the entrance. It is for supervised time outdoors, not a replacement for indoor housing.

What you get

  • Off-ground floor. The raised base keeps their resting area away from wet grass and mud.
  • A ramp, not a step. An access ramp leads up to the entrance; ask us its dimensions to check it suits your dog.
  • Arched doorway. A shaped entrance gives the kennel its sheltered form.
  • Wipe-clean shell. The plastic wipes down with a damp cloth and mild soap after muddy days.

Best for

Supervised garden time for dogs a ramp suits. Give them water and safe shade, and bring them indoors in extreme weather: a kennel won't stop overheating.

Size and fit

Ask us for the internal floor, doorway height, ramp and supported load measurements before ordering. Your dog should be able to use the ramp, then stand, turn and lie down comfortably inside.

Care

Wipe the shell with a damp cloth and mild soap. Follow the separate care label for any cushion, keep soft furnishings under cover, and let all parts dry before use.
