A big dog gets all of themselves on the bed, back legs included, with a bolster to press against. At 122 × 89cm it has around 70% more floor area than a typical 90 × 70cm large bed, with memory foam underneath.

Hawthorn is an XL orthopaedic bolster dog bed, 122 × 89cm and 18cm deep, with memory foam and an egg-crate layer, a U-shaped bolster on three sides, a textured plush surface and a waterproof inner layer.

What you get

  • Bolster on three sides. The U-shaped surround gives a chin, a back or a hip something to lean into.
  • Open front. No wall to step over, and the shape never feels boxed in.
  • Memory foam underneath. Paired with an egg-crate profiled layer, under a textured plush sleeping surface.
  • Cover opens on three sides. Easy to get off, so it actually gets washed.
  • Waterproof inner layer. Protects the foam underneath.

Best for

Big dogs who lean against a surround, and owners who are tired of fighting a cover back on.

Size and fit

These are outside measurements and the bolster takes up some of that. Measure your dog lying on their side, nose to base of tail, and add 15–20cm. Not sure? Ask us with their breed and weight.

Care

Open the cover, remove it and wash it following the sewn-in care label. Air-dry fully before refitting. Never wash or soak the foam.

A bed is for comfort, not treatment. If your dog is in pain, recovering or finding it hard to move, ask your vet about the right place for them to rest.
