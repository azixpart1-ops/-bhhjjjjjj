Your dog gets raised, cushioned sides to lean into, and a bed smart enough to stay in the living room with you. A bed you like the look of stays in the room where your dog wants to be, not out in the hall.

Magpie is a bolster dog bed with a classic houndstooth exterior, raised cushioned sides, soft fibre filling and a removable, washable cover. Four sizes, Small to XL.

What you get

  • Raised sides. A cushioned edge around the resting area to rest a chin against.
  • Soft fibre filling. Yielding rather than firm. If your dog needs firm support, choose a memory foam bed.
  • Houndstooth throughout. A pattern that belongs in a living room rather than a utility room.
  • A washable cover. It comes off for cleaning, following the sewn-in care label.

Best for

Dogs who like an edge to lean on, in homes where the bed sits in a room you have decorated.

Size and fit

The raised sides take up part of the outside size, so measure your dog curled up as well as stretched out. Ask us for exact measurements, or which of the four sizes to take.

Care

Remove the cover and wash it following the sewn-in care label. Air-dry fully before refitting.
