Maple Orthopaedic Chaise Dog Bed

Cover on two sides and open on two, so a watchful dog can settle without losing the view.

A watchful dog gets their back covered and the door in sight, without having to leave the bed. Shape, not comfort, is why most beds get abandoned. Maple has the shape a watchful dog will use, with layered profiled foam underneath.

Maple is an orthopaedic chaise dog bed, 112 × 84cm and 15cm deep, with layered foam under an egg-crate top, an L-shaped bolster along two sides and a soft flannel sleeping surface.

What you get

  • L-shaped bolster. Cover along two sides, with a chin rest at the corner.
  • Two open sides. Nothing blocking their view of the room, and nothing to step over.
  • Layered foam. An egg-crate top spreads their weight instead of compressing flat.
  • Flannel to the edge. A soft flannel sleeping surface across the whole open area.

Best for

Watchful dogs who want their back covered and the door in sight, and dogs who like to stretch out over an open edge.

Size and fit

Two sides are open and usable right to the edge. Measure your dog lying on their side, nose to base of tail, and add 15–20cm. Not sure? Ask us with their breed and weight.

Care

Follow the sewn-in care label on the cover. Air-dry fully before refitting. Never wash or soak the foam.

A bed is for comfort, not treatment. If your dog is in pain, recovering or finding it hard to move, ask your vet about the right place for them to rest.
