An older dog keeps the full surround they like to press into, and steps in over a lowered front. The waterproof liner and the low front decide how many years you get from a bed. Both are invisible in photos, so cheaper beds leave them out.

Juniper is an XL orthopaedic dog sofa bed, 106.5 × 81.5cm and 16.5cm deep, with a profiled egg-crate foam core, bolsters on four sides, a lowered entry, a flannel cover, a waterproof liner and a non-slip base.

What you get

  • Lowered entry. The front section drops, so stepping in takes nothing out of them.
  • Bolsters on four sides. Something to lean into whichever way they turn.
  • Egg-crate foam core. Profiled foam that spreads their weight rather than flattening.
  • Waterproof liner. Accidents and wet coats stop before they reach the foam.
  • Non-slip base. No skating across the kitchen floor when they get in.

Best for

Older dogs who hesitate at the edge of a bed, and dogs who like to press into a surround on every side.

Size and fit

The bolsters take up part of the outside footprint. Measure your dog lying on their side, nose to base of tail, and add 15–20cm. Not sure? Ask us with their breed and weight.

Care

Remove the cover and wash it following the sewn-in care label. Wipe the liner. Air-dry fully before refitting. Never wash or soak the foam.

A bed is for comfort, not treatment. If your dog is in pain, recovering or finding it hard to move, ask your vet about the right place for them to rest.
