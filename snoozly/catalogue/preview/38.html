Wherever your dog lies down, even right at the edge, there's foam under them and no sinking through to the floor. Two layers doing two jobs: memory foam on top contours to them, and a firmer base stops them bottoming out.

Cypress is an edge-to-edge dog mattress: a contouring memory foam top layer over a firmer foam base, with no walls, in Medium, Large and XL up to 140 × 100cm. The cover comes off to wash.

What you get

  • Two layers, not one. Contouring memory foam over a firmer supporting base.
  • Foam to every edge. No soft rim to roll off and no dead space around the outside.
  • No walls to climb. A level mattress they can step onto from any side, at any age.
  • Room to sprawl. Three sizes, up to 140 × 100cm, for dogs who never fit a standard mattress.

Measure your dog lying on their side, nose to base of tail, and add 15–20cm. Every centimetre is usable sleeping area, so there's no need to allow for bolsters. Not sure? Ask us with their breed and weight.

Care

Remove the cover and wash it following the sewn-in care label. Air-dry fully before refitting. Never wash or soak the foam.

A bed is for comfort, not treatment. If your dog is in pain, recovering or finding it hard to move, ask your vet about the right place for them to rest.
