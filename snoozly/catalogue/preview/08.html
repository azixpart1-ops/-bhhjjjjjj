Elm XL Orthopaedic Dog Sofa Bed

A plush sofa bed with a waterproof liner that keeps a bad night out of the foam.

Your dog gets a plush sofa bed to lean into, and accidents stop at a waterproof liner, not in the foam. Plenty of beds are thrown out still sound but smelling. Here everything above the liner washes, and the foam stays protected beneath it.

Elm is an XL orthopaedic dog sofa bed, 106.5 × 81.5cm, with cushioned sofa-style sides, a profiled egg-crate foam base, a plush sleeping surface, a waterproof inner liner and a removable, machine-washable cover.

What you get

  • A waterproof inner liner. Accidents and wet coats stop at the liner instead of soaking into the core.
  • Sofa-style sides. Cushioned edges to lean against or rest a chin on.
  • Plush on profiled foam. A plush surface over egg-crate foam that spreads their weight rather than compressing flat.
  • Machine-washable cover. It comes off and goes in the machine, following the sewn-in care label.

Best for

Dogs who like a sofa edge to lean on, in homes where wet coats and the odd accident are part of life.

Size and fit

One size, XL. Measure your dog lying on their side, nose to base of tail, and add 15–20cm; the sides take up part of the footprint. Not sure? Ask us with their breed and weight.

Care

Remove the cover and machine wash it following the sewn-in care label. Wipe the liner. Air-dry fully before refitting, and never wash or soak the foam.

A bed is for comfort, not treatment. If your dog is in pain, recovering or finding it hard to move, ask your vet about the right place for them to rest.
