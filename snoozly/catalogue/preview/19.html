What you get

  • Room to stretch. An open mattress with all four sides clear, so they can lie however they like.
  • Soft, not firm. Virgin fibre filling is gentler underfoot than a firm foam slab. This is not a memory foam mattress.
  • Faux fur on top. Soft faux fur where they sleep, with herringbone on the outside.
  • Easy to live with. Washable covers come off, leather handles help you move it, and the base is non-slip.

Best for

Sprawlers who like to lie full length and prefer soft fibre cushioning and faux fur to a firm foam mattress.
