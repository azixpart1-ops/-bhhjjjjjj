Walnut comes in one size. Measure your dog lying on their side, nose to base of tail, and add 15–20cm, then ask us for the exact measurements and whether it fits.

Care

Follow the sewn-in care label on the cover. Let it air-dry fully before refitting, and never wash or soak the foam.

A bed is for comfort, not treatment. If your dog is in pain, recovering or finding it hard to move, ask your vet about the right place for them to rest.
