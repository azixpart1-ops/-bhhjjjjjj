Your dog can turn, knead and burrow until they drop, with cushioned walls to press into and sherpa moulding round them. Upholstery-weight stonewashed fabric and a proper sherpa cushion, and every cover zips off and washes at 30°C.

Linnet is a high-walled bolster nest bed in green stonewashed fabric, with a deep sherpa-fleece cushion, springy virgin fibre filling, a non-slip base and zipped covers that wash at 30°C. Five sizes, XS to XL.

What you get

  • Cushioned walls all round. Somewhere for a chin or a back, whichever way they turn.
  • A deep sherpa cushion. Soft and warm where they actually lie.
  • Soft, not firm. Springy virgin fibre filling by design. This is a nest, not a firm foam mattress.
  • Covers that come off. Zipped, removable and machine washable at 30°C.
  • Toy breeds to retrievers. Five sizes, so a small dog isn't lost in a bed built for a Labrador.

Best for

Dogs who turn, knead and burrow before settling, from toy breeds to retrievers. Not for dogs who need firm support.
