Elder is a memory foam dog sofa with raised, sofa-style sides to rest a head on, in Medium, Large and XL. The outer cover comes off for machine washing.

What you get

  • Memory foam where they lie. A memory foam base forms the resting surface.
  • Room to prop a chin. Sofa-style sides give them somewhere to rest their head and keep watch.
  • Sized to sit beside you. Made to fit next to your chair, so they stay close enough to hear you move.
  • Machine-washable cover. The outer cover comes off for washing. The foam stays out of the machine.

Best for

Dogs who sleep with their head up on the sofa arm, the step or your foot.

Care

Remove the cover and wash it following the sewn-in care label. Air-dry fully before refitting. Never wash or soak the foam.

A bed is for comfort, not treatment. If your dog is in pain, recovering or finding it hard to move, ask your vet about the right place for them to rest.
