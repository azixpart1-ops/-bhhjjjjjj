Harbour is an XL elevated dog bed: a 150 × 95cm mesh deck with bolsters on three sides, on a powder-coated steel frame with non-slip rubber feet. It snaps together without tools.

What you get

  • Air underneath. The mesh deck sits clear of the floor, so heat moves away instead of building up.
  • Three sides to lean on. Bolsters for a chin, a back or a sideways flop, with an open side to step in.
  • Built for a big dog. A powder-coated steel frame under a 150 × 95cm deck.
  • Non-slip rubber feet. It stays put on tiles, laminate or decking.
  • No tools needed. The frame snaps together.

Best for

Large and giant breeds who run hot but still want something to lean against, indoors or in the garden.

Size and fit

The bolsters take up part of the outside footprint. Measure your dog lying on their side, nose to base of tail, and add 15–20cm. Not sure? Ask us with their breed and weight.

Care

Wipe or rinse the mesh and frame and let it dry fully. Check the feet and joints occasionally, and keep it on a level surface.
