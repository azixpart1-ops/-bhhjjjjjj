### Google's spec constrains exactly the tactics the brief's 'manipulation' framing would otherwise reach for. These are hard feed rules, not style preferences.

## Hard rules

- Every factual claim in the rewritten copy must trace to PawLunova's existing product data. The brief says 'all descriptions are accurate' — so treat the current descriptions as the fact base and do not introduce any spec, dimension, material, certification or capability that is not already there. If a fact is missing, flag it for the client rather than inventing it.
- Do not fabricate reviews, ratings, star counts, testimonials, review counts, or 'X customers bought this' figures. The store has ZERO published reviews. Writing an AggregateRating into the theme or inventing a testimonial would be a banned misrepresentation under Google's Shopping policies and a criminal-standard offence under the UK DMCC Act 2024 (fake reviews) — it risks the entire Merchant Center account, which is the opposite of the stated goal of ranking in Google Shopping. Leave every proof slot empty until real reviews exist, and prioritise standing up review capture.
- Do not fabricate urgency or scarcity. No countdown timers, no 'only 3 left', no 'sale ends tonight' unless the stock figure or the end date is literally true. False scarcity is a banned practice under the UK CPRs/DMCC and under Google's policies.

Do not say 'clinically proven', 'vet recommended', 'veterinarian approved', 'vet designed' or cite any study — Big Barker has a University of Pennsylvania study and Berkeley has Canine Arthritis Management approval; PawLunova has neither and must not borrow or imply them.
